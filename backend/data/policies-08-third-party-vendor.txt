POLICY: THIRD-PARTY AND VENDOR API USAGE
===========================================

Policy ID: POL-VEN-008
Effective Date: 2026-03-01
Owner: Platform Engineering Team
Review Cycle: Annual

Purpose
-------
Governs the selection, monitoring, and cost management of third-party services the system depends on, primarily OpenRouter for LLM access and Pinecone for vector storage.

Scope
-----
Applies to any external API or managed service integrated into the backend.

Policy Statements
------------------
1. New third-party dependencies must be evaluated for data residency, uptime SLA, and cost before integration.
2. OpenRouter usage should default to a cost-aware routing strategy (e.g., "openrouter/auto") unless a specific task requires a fixed model.
3. Monthly spend on OpenRouter and Pinecone must be reviewed by the Platform Engineering Team, with alerts configured for spend exceeding budget thresholds.
4. Vendor outages must be monitored via status pages, and a fallback behavior (e.g., a friendly error message) must exist for when a vendor is unavailable.
5. Contracts or terms of service changes from vendors must be reviewed for compliance implications before accepting.
6. API keys for third-party vendors must be rotated at least every 90 days or immediately upon suspected compromise.
7. A single point of contact should be designated for each vendor relationship to streamline support requests.

Enforcement
-----------
Vendor spend and health are reviewed monthly. Unbudgeted spend spikes trigger an automatic review by the Platform Engineering Team.
