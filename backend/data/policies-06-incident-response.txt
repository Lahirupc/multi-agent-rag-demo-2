POLICY: INCIDENT RESPONSE
============================

Policy ID: POL-OPS-006
Effective Date: 2026-02-15
Owner: Platform Engineering Team
Review Cycle: Annual

Purpose
-------
Establishes a consistent process for detecting, reporting, and resolving incidents affecting the availability or correctness of the Multi-Agent RAG Demo.

Scope
-----
Applies to outages, degraded performance, data integrity issues, and security events involving the frontend, backend, vector store, or LLM provider.

Policy Statements
------------------
1. Any team member who observes a service disruption must open an incident report using the standard template within 30 minutes of detection.
2. Incidents are classified by severity: Critical (full outage), High (major feature broken), Medium (degraded but usable), Low (cosmetic or minor).
3. Critical and High severity incidents require a dedicated incident channel and an assigned incident commander until resolution.
4. A root cause analysis must be completed and documented for every Critical or High severity incident within five business days of resolution.
5. Incident reports must include a timeline of detection, mitigation, and resolution, along with follow-up action items and owners.
6. Communication to stakeholders during an active incident should occur at least every 30 minutes for Critical severity issues.
7. Post-incident action items must be tracked to completion; unresolved items are reviewed in the following sprint planning meeting.

Enforcement
-----------
The on-call engineer is responsible for initiating this process. Failure to file an incident report for a qualifying event is reviewed by the engineering lead.
