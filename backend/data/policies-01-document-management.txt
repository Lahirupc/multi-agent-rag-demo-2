POLICY: DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT
============================

Policy ID: POL-DOC-001
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Owner: Platform Engineering Team
Review Cycle: Annual

Purpose
-------
This policy governs how internal knowledge base documents used by the Multi-Agent RAG Demo are created, stored, and retired. It ensures that content ingested into Pinecone remains accurate, well-formatted, and traceable to an owner.

Scope
-----
Applies to all text-based source documents placed in backend/data/, including policies, runbooks, architecture documents, incident reports, product specifications, and meeting notes.

Policy Statements
------------------
1. All internal documents must be maintained in the backend/data/ directory in plain text (.txt) or Markdown (.md) format for compatibility with the ingestion pipeline.
2. Every document must include a clear title and, where applicable, a document ID, owner, and effective date near the top of the file.
3. Documents must not exceed roughly 5,000 words; longer topics should be split into multiple focused documents to keep vector chunks coherent.
4. Superseded documents must be removed from backend/data/ rather than left in place with outdated content, since the ingestion pipeline treats all files in the directory as current.
5. Any change to a document's content requires a corresponding re-ingestion into Pinecone to keep the vector store in sync with the source of truth.
6. Document owners are responsible for reviewing their documents at least once per year, or immediately after a significant process or architecture change.

Enforcement
-----------
Pull requests that add or modify files under backend/data/ must be reviewed by at least one member of the Platform Engineering Team before merge. Documents that do not follow this policy's formatting conventions will be returned for revision.
